What it does

Everything about your PRs, one click from the menu bar.

No dashboards to check. It sits in your menu bar and tells you exactly what changed.

Tracks the right PRs

PRs assigned to you, review requests aimed at you or your team, PRs you authored, and PRs you've already reviewed — they stay until merged. Paste a link or owner/repo#123 to watch anything else.

Every reviewer's verdict

See who approved, who asked for changes and who's still pending — including your own review, and re-review requests. A GitHub-style badge shows Open, Draft, Conflicts, Merged or Closed.

Review summaries, your rules

Point GitWall at any review bot — or person — that posts a severity table. Choose the authors, an optional marker like Findings Summary, and your labels (P0, P1, P2… or High, Medium, Low). Non-zero counts show right on the row.

Unresolved comments

A comment badge counts open review threads. Click it to read each one, jump to it on GitHub, or resolve them all at once.

A real Mac app layout

Sidebar sections — Inbox, Needs you, My PRs, Reviews, Watching, Pinned, Ignored — that collapse to an icon rail. Compact rows expand one at a time. Merged PRs clear themselves after 30 minutes (configurable).

Liquid glass & accents

Pick Standard or a translucent Liquid glass theme, choose an accent colour, and enjoy a smooth spring-in every time the popover opens.

What's new

The biggest GitWall update yet.

Rebuilt around a sidebar, with far more of what's happening on each PR visible at a glance.

  • Every reviewer's verdict — approved, changes requested, commented or pending, including yours.
  • One "your review" tag — your turn, re-requested, you requested changes, or you approved.
  • Reviewed & team requests stay on the list until the PR merges.
  • PR state badges — Open, Draft, Conflicts, Merged, Closed.
  • Merged PRs clear themselves after 30 minutes (configurable).
  • Custom review summaries from any bot or person, with a live rule tester.
  • Unresolved comments — read each thread, jump to it, or resolve all.
  • Sidebar sections that collapse to an icon rail with tooltips.
  • Compact rows that expand one at a time; author shown next to the PR number.
  • Update branch from the menu bar — then get notified when the checks pass or fail.
  • Two ways to ignore from a notification: the whole PR, or just the menu-bar count.
  • Liquid glass theme, accent colours and a spring-in opening animation.
Colour means one thing

Whose move it is, at a glance.

Repo headers carry up to two badges — red for changes you owe, yellow for reviews you owe. The menu-bar count is only the red ones — and any PR can be left out of it from a notification.

Changes requestedOn your PR — or "you requested changes" on theirs.
Your turnYour review is requested, or re-requested (↻) after you reviewed.
ApprovedReady to merge — or "you approved", kept visible until it merges.
ConflictsThe branch conflicts with its base.
MergedLingers for 30 minutes, then clears itself.
How it works

Build it once. It polls quietly in the background.

01

Sign in

Picks up your gh CLI token automatically, or paste a personal access token with repo scope. Stored in the login Keychain — never on disk.

02

It polls in the background

Every 120s by default (30s–30min in settings). Each poll costs a handful of GitHub API calls, well inside normal rate limits.

03

Act only when pinged

Click a notification to jump to the PR, or use its options to ignore the PR completely or just keep it out of the menu-bar count.

Install

Build from source — it's a two-minute build.

No Homebrew tap yet, so GitWall is installed by building the Swift package locally.

1
Prerequisites

macOS 14 or later, and Xcode 16+ (or the Swift 6 command line tools) for swift build.

2
Clone and build

Builds a universal (arm64 + x86_64) GitWall.app and copies it to /Applications.

git clone https://github.com/Vahsir7/GitWall.git cd GitWall chmod +x build.sh ./build.sh --install
3
Launch and sign in

Open GitWall from /Applications — it appears in the menu bar with no Dock icon. If the gh CLI is installed and logged in, it authenticates automatically; otherwise open the gear icon and paste a personal access token.

4
Optional: start at login

System Settings → General → Login Items → add GitWall.
